# Review of issues #891–#917

Reviewed against `4189ba52` on 2026-09-17. The implementation commits below were pushed directly to `dev` at the product owner's request. No deployment is claimed.

23 reports were validated and fixed. Two describe existing intended or protected behavior (#899 and #915). The product owner confirmed that users should be able to choose any Administrator-enabled sign-in method (#892), which the current implementation supports, and deferred restoring Request type Targets (#914). No product decision remains pending.

The product owner also clarified that a Business Owner must automatically be a Contract or Matter member. Assignment now adds membership in the same transaction, existing owners are backfilled with audit entries, and a current Business Owner must be reassigned or cleared before their membership can be removed. Reassignment retains the former owner's membership until explicitly removed. Confidential audience-management permissions still apply when choosing a new owner.
