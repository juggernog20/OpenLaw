# Work on a shared Contract or Matter

Add business information, supporting Documents, and comments to the Contract or Matter your Legal team has shared with you.

## Before you start

- Sign in with a Contributor account. A Business User's Portal account has different access.
- Ask Legal to add you to the specific Contract or Matter team. A link or related record alone does not grant access.
- Open **Contracts** or **Matters** in the app navigation and select the record. You can also follow its link or a Task from Home.
- Check the record number and title. An archived record remains read-only until a permitted Legal Team Member or Administrator restores it.

## Update business information

### On a Contract

1. Open **Overview** to update permitted value controls such as **Amount**, **Currency**, and **Cadence**, or **Effective date**.
2. Open **Fields** to edit the business Fields supplied by that Contract's type. Your organization chooses their names and required values.
3. Leave the edited field to save it. **Amount**, **Currency**, and **Cadence** save together when you leave that group of controls, not when you move between them. Wait for the saved indication, then reload the record and check the value before relying on it.

The Contract's title, type, Owner, parties, Description, Priority, Risk, Status, and legal Fields are not Contributor edits. The page states **Legal-managed details are read-only. You can edit the business Fields available to you.**

### On a Matter

1. Open **Overview**.
2. Update **Description** or the business Fields under **Custom fields**, where present.
3. Leave the edited field to save it. Wait for the saved indication, then reload and check the value.

The Matter's title, type, Matter Manager, Priority, Risk, and Status remain legal-managed. The Fields available on one type may differ from another type's Fields.

## Supply a supporting Document

1. Open the record's **Documents** section.
2. Select **Upload**, then **Choose files**, and select a supporting file.
3. Review the file and available details. Add a **Note** when it helps explain the paper, then select **Upload**.
4. Wait for the upload to finish and confirm that the Document appears. Open it to check the filename and Version. Preview or text processing may finish later than the upload.

To supply a later Version of a supporting Document, open its **Actions for** menu, select **Add version**, choose the replacement file, and select **Upload**. Check that the new Version appears in the same Document chain. Earlier Versions remain.

A Contributor cannot append to a Contract's primary Document, make a Document primary, set an Executed pin, change confidentiality, archive Documents, or manage folders. Ask Legal to handle those actions. A comment attachment is conversation paper; uploading in **Documents** creates a managed supporting Document.

## Join the conversation

1. Select **Comments** on the record.
2. Choose **Working team** for the working group or **Full thread** when the message is suitable for the originating requester too.
3. Write in **New comment** and select **Comment**.
4. Check that the comment appears under your name in the selected tier.

**Legal only** is unavailable to Contributors. A mention does not grant record access. Ask Legal to arrange access before trying to include someone who is not offered.

## Know which work stays with Legal

You can read the permitted Tasks, Key dates, and relationships on a shared record. A Task assigned to you does not let you edit the checklist or mark it done in OpenLaw. Tell the responsible Legal Team Member when your work is ready.

Ask Legal to change Status, end or close work, change the Owner or Matter Manager, manage teams, edit legal Fields, manage relationships, request Approvals, or send for signature. Contributors cannot create a new Contract or Matter from the Contracts or Matters lists or promote their own access.

## If it does not work

If saving reports an error, keep your intended value, follow the displayed correction or retry instruction, and check the saved result. Do not assume that leaving the field saved it successfully.

If an upload fails, check its error and retry after correcting the cause. Check the Documents list first so you do not create a duplicate of a file that already uploaded. Ask Legal about an unavailable primary-Document action.

If the record disappears or becomes unavailable, ask Legal to check your team entry. Removing it stops access on the next read. Restoring a browser tab or copying a link does not restore access.
