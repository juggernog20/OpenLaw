# Find Documents in the repository

Use **Documents** to find paper across the owning records you can reach, then open the matching current Version on its record.

## Before you start

Legal Team Members and Administrators can find reached Contract, Matter, Entity, and Knowledge Documents. Contributors see only permitted Contract and Matter paper. Business Users use the Business Portal's Request and Knowledge surfaces instead.

The repository is a flat view. Uploads and folder management start on the [owning record](document-versions.md). Confidentiality and record access still apply to results and filter choices.

## Search and narrow the list

1. Open **Documents** from navigation. **Recent** shows up to five Documents whose current Versions were uploaded most recently; it does not mean recently opened.
2. Use the visible filters above the list. Choose **Owner** to narrow to **Contracts**, **Matters**, **Entities**, or **Knowledge**.
3. To choose one owning record, use **Record**, type its reference or title, and select the match. A **Folder** filter becomes available for one Contract, Matter, or Entity; **Record root** means paper outside its folders.
4. Combine **Format**, **Kind**, **Counterparty**, **Uploader**, **Uploaded from**, and **Uploaded to** as needed. Active filters must all match. The upload dates include their stated endpoints and refer to the current Version's upload date.
5. Remove an individual filter or select **Clear all** to widen the results. Recent documents is hidden while filters are active.

Kind, uploader, format, and dates describe the current Version. A Counterparty filter follows its Contract relationship; it does not create Counterparties on Matters or Entities. Knowledge Document folders are unavailable because Knowledge Folders organize items instead.

Use [saved views and list controls](search-and-views.md) for reusable layouts. For words inside an earlier Version or an OCR'd scan, use global search; repository filters are not a complete search of every historical Version's contents.

## Open the matching paper

Select the Document row's open action or name. Its owning record opens with the current Version selected. Check the reader's Version number; expand the Document's chain on the record to select an earlier round. The owning-record reference identifies where the paper belongs.

## Missing or archived Documents

A Legal Team Member or Administrator can turn on **Show archived** to include archived Documents. Use the offered **Restore** action when permitted, or open the owning record and follow [archive and restore](archive-and-delete-documents.md). Restore the owning record first if it is archived.

Contributors are not offered archived Document management. A hidden record or Document does not become visible through a filter, saved view, or direct link. If the list is empty, clear filters, check the intended owner and current Version properties, then ask the responsible Legal Team Member about access or archive status. A permanently deleted Document cannot be restored from this view.
