# Publish Knowledge for the right audience

Choose when a Knowledge Item becomes available to Portal readers, and withdraw it when its guidance is no longer ready to use.

## Before you start

Sign in as a Legal Team Member or Administrator and open the [Knowledge Item](create-knowledge.md). Both roles can publish; there is no separate curator approval step. Review the guidance and every Document, including supporting files, before changing availability.

| Item condition                    | Staff Knowledge access                                                               | Business Portal access                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Draft                             | Legal Team Members and Administrators                                                | Unavailable                                                                       |
| Published, Legal Only             | Legal Team Members and Administrators                                                | Unavailable                                                                       |
| Published, Everyone, not archived | Legal Team Members and Administrators                                                | Signed-in Portal readers can read guidance and download current Document Versions |
| Archived                          | Legal Team Members and Administrators can open a saved staff link to read or restore | Unavailable                                                                       |

**Everyone** still requires a Portal session; it does not create an anonymous public page. Contributors have no staff Knowledge authoring access. Organization Knowledge and OpenLaw product **Help** have separate content and search.

## Publish and check the Portal

1. Set **Audience** to **Everyone** if the item is intended for Portal readers, or keep **Legal Only** for the legal team's library.
2. Open **Knowledge Item actions** and select **Publish**. The **Draft** marker disappears. A live published item with **Everyone** shows **On the portal**.
3. Ask an Administrator to add an internal Knowledge link to the Portal home or the appropriate [request form](request-forms.md). Publishing alone does not add a link to either location.
4. In a separate signed-in Business Portal session, open the configured link under **Before you submit**. Check the title, guidance, and current downloads for both primary and supporting Documents. The [Portal reading guide](portal-knowledge.md) explains this view.

Portal readers see the primary Document first and guidance after the files. They cannot browse the staff Knowledge library, edit the item, or choose earlier Versions. A new Document Version or a guidance edit becomes available on the published item without another Publish action. Files previously downloaded to a computer remain there and do not update automatically.

## Withdraw or restrict an item

1. To return an item to draft, open **Knowledge Item actions** and select **Unpublish**. To keep it published for Legal, change **Audience** to **Legal Only** instead.
2. If **Remove this from the portal?** appears, read the number of affected links. Select **Cancel** to keep availability unchanged, or **Continue** to proceed.
3. Check the resulting **Draft** marker or **Audience**. Reopen the Portal home or form and check that the link is gone. A previously saved Portal article link and its file links must no longer open the content.

The configured links remain in Settings for an Administrator to repair or reuse. Unpublishing, restricting the audience, or archiving blocks later Portal reads and downloads; it cannot remove a file someone already downloaded. Reopening an unavailable internal Knowledge link may return the reader to the Portal home.

To make the item available again, set **Audience** to **Everyone** and **Publish** a draft. Existing configured links become available again when the item is live, published, and for Everyone. Check from the Portal after the change.

## Archive and restore

1. Save the item's staff page address before archiving. The ordinary Knowledge list excludes archived items and has no archived-items toggle.
2. Open **Knowledge Item actions** and select **Archive**. In **Archive Knowledge Item**, optionally select a live item under **Replaced by**, or keep **No replacement**. Select **Archive**.
3. Check **Archived** and the disabled editing controls. Guidance and Documents remain on the item, but Portal article and file access are removed.
4. To restore, open the saved staff address, then select **Knowledge Item actions**, **Restore**. Confirm the item reappears in the library and its fields can be edited.

Restoring preserves the item's previous publication state and audience. An item archived while published for Everyone becomes Portal-readable again when restored, including through its existing configured links. If it should return only as a draft, unpublish it before archiving. Review the Portal result after restoration.

Naming a replacement records the relationship; it does not redirect the old Portal address or rewrite its configured links. Ask an Administrator to point those links at the intended replacement and verify that the replacement is itself published for Everyone.

## Check a missing item or file

Confirm the item is not archived, its audience is **Everyone**, and it is published. Then check the configured link's placement and the reader's Portal sign-in. These conditions apply to supporting Documents as well as the primary Document; saving a direct download address does not bypass them.

If a publication or audience change fails, read the message, reload the record, and check the saved state before retrying. If the item opens but a current file fails, follow [Document recovery](document-previews.md). An unavailable or archived replacement cannot be selected; reopen the archive dialog to refresh its choices.
