# Change a Matter Status or archive it

A Matter's Status has a configurable name and a fixed **open** or **closed** Category. Closing takes work off active surfaces while keeping it editable. Archiving hides it from default views and freezes changes.

## Before you start

Use a Legal Team Member or Administrator account that can read the Matter. Contributors can read its Status but cannot change it, close or reopen it, or archive or restore it. An archived Matter must be restored before ordinary changes.

## Change Status within the current Category

Open the Matter and use **Status** beside its title. This control offers live Statuses in the Matter's current Category. Choose the appropriate label and wait for the save result.

Your organization may rename Statuses. A label does not change its Category: a Status called “Resolved” could still belong to the open Category. Use the available **Close matter** or **Reopen matter** action to understand and change the lifecycle, and ask an Administrator about [Status configuration](types-statuses-fields.md) if a label is confusing.

## Close or reopen the Matter

Select **Close matter** to move from open to closed. In the confirmation dialog, choose the intended **Closed Status**. Read **Open child Matters** if shown: those children will stay open. An inaccessible child appears as **Restricted Matter** without its identity. Select **Close matter** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the current Status.

Closing records **Closed** on Overview and removes the Matter from the default open list and active deadline surfaces. Its Tasks, Key dates, Documents, Fields, and relationships remain, and permitted users can continue editing them. Closing does not complete Tasks, close children, end linked Contracts, or grant access to related work.

To resume active work, select **Reopen matter**, choose an **Open Status**, and confirm **Reopen matter**. Reopening clears the current Closed date and preserves the original **Opened** date. Still-future Key dates can feed active deadline surfaces again. Moving between Statuses within one Category preserves those lifecycle dates. **History** retains the transitions.

To find closed work, open **Matters**, use **Filter** and enable **Show closed**, and check any other active filters or saved view. You can also follow an existing Matter link if you still have access.

## Archive or restore

Select **Archive** on the Matter, read the confirmation, and select **Archive** again. Use **Cancel** if you only meant to close normal completed work. Archiving keeps the M- reference and History while removing the Matter from default lists and preventing ordinary changes.

To restore it, find the Matter with **Show archived** in the Matters filters or follow its existing link. If it is also closed, include **Show closed** as well. Select **Restore**, then confirm **Restore** in the dialog.

Restoring makes the Matter editable again; it does not reopen a closed Matter or change the lifecycle of children or linked Contracts. Reopen separately if needed. If a write is refused because another person archived the Matter, read its current state and restore it before trying again. Review [finding work](find-your-work.md) when filters still hide the record.
