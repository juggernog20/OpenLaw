# Relate, end, and archive Contracts

Connect related work without copying access, then choose the appropriate lifecycle or archive action.

## Before you start

Sign in as a Legal Team Member or Administrator with access to the Contract. Both records must be available for a new link. A relationship does not copy team membership, Fields, Documents, dates, Tasks, confidentiality, or lifecycle changes.

## Relate Contracts

On **Overview**, find **Related contracts**. Select **Set parent** to place this Contract under another, or **Add link** to choose **Related**, **Renews**, or **Amends**. Search by number or title, inspect the selected C- reference, and confirm the action. Cancel leaves the relationship unchanged.

Check the direction: this Contract **Renews** or **Amends** the selected Contract. The other record reads **Renewed by** or **Amended by**. Parent and child are separate from typed links. Use **Remove parent** or **Remove link** on the corresponding row to remove only that relationship.

The app refuses self-links, duplicate typed links, and a parent choice that creates a cycle. Choose a different record or inspect the existing relationship. If **Flag as confidential?** appears after linking, read it before choosing. **Flag as confidential** sets the other Contract's Confidential flag. **No, leave it open** changes nothing. The link itself does not change either flag or grant access. A reader who cannot reach a related Contract sees **Restricted contract** instead of its details.

## Link a Matter

On **Overview**, find **Matter** and select **Link to Matter**. Search and inspect the M- reference, then select **Link**. A Contract belongs to at most one Matter through this link; select **Unlink** on the existing Matter before moving it to another. A confidentiality mismatch is advice to review the records. It does not align their flags automatically.

See [Create and maintain a Contract](create-contract.md) for the optional Matter link at creation, and [Create a Matter and use a template](create-matter.md) for the Matter workflow.

## End or reopen a Contract

Use the Stage control to choose a Status mapped to **Ended**, such as Expired or Terminated if your organisation uses those names. Check the Status and Activity. Ending records the lifecycle change; the Contract remains editable for later paper and notes.

The default Contracts view excludes ended work. Open **Filter** and enable **Show ended**. Review any other active filters or saved view if the Contract remains hidden. To reopen it, select the appropriate non-ended Status. This is another recorded Status change; it does not delete the earlier history. [Change a Contract Status](contract-stages.md) covers the control and approval warning.

## Archive or restore

Open **Contract actions** and select **Archive** for a record you want out of the working list. Check the **Archived** marker and read-only message. Archiving is a separate action from ending and does not select an Ended Status.

To recover it, open **Filter** in the Contracts list and enable **Show archived**. Also enable **Show ended** if the Contract is Ended. Open the Contract and choose **Contract actions** → **Restore**. You can also open its known C- address to restore it. Check that the archived marker is gone and editing is available. Its Status remains what it was, so a restored Ended Contract can still be excluded by the ended-work filter.

Archiving and restoring do not archive or restore linked work. If a link or action is unavailable, check both records' access and archive state and read the refusal before retrying. [Search and saved views](search-and-views.md) explains finding records hidden by filters.
