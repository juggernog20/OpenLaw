# Change a Matter Status or archive it

A Matter's Status has a configurable name and a fixed **open** or **closed** Category. Closing takes work off active surfaces while keeping it editable. Archiving hides it from default views and freezes changes.

## Before you start

Use a Legal Team Member or Administrator account that can read the Matter. Business Users can read its Status in the Portal but cannot change it, close or reopen it, or archive or restore it. An archived Matter must be restored before ordinary changes. On an archived Matter, the Status strip shows the current group without a **Move to** menu.

## Change Status within the current Category

Open the Matter. The pill beside its title shows the exact Status name. The **Status** strip at the right of the header shows four groups: **Open**, **In progress**, **Waiting**, and **Closed**. Select a group to open its **Move to** menu, then choose a Status. A Status in the same Category saves at once. Wait for the save result.

Your organization may rename Statuses. A label does not change its Category. For example, a Status called "Resolved" can still belong to the open Category. The group that lists a Status shows its Category. Statuses under **Closed** are closed. Statuses under **Open**, **In progress**, and **Waiting** are open. Ask an Administrator about [Status configuration](types-statuses-fields.md) if a label is confusing.

## Close or reopen the Matter

To close an open Matter, select **Closed** in the Status strip and choose the intended closed Status. The close dialog opens for that Status. Enter a **Closing note**. The note is required, with a maximum of 2,000 characters. Read **Open child Matters** if shown. Those children will stay open. An inaccessible child appears as **Restricted Matter** without its identity. Select **Close matter** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the current Status.

Closing records the **Closed** date on Overview, keeps the note under **Closing note** in **History**, and removes the Matter from the default open list and active deadline surfaces. Its Tasks, Key dates, Documents, Fields, and relationships remain, and permitted users can continue editing them. Closing does not complete Tasks, close children, end linked Contracts, or grant access to related work.

To resume active work, select **Open**, **In progress**, or **Waiting** in the Status strip and choose an open Status. The reopen dialog uses that Status. Select **Reopen matter** to confirm. Reopening clears the current Closed date, so Overview shows **Still open**, and preserves the original **Opened** date. Still-future Key dates can feed active deadline surfaces again. Moving between Statuses within one Category preserves those lifecycle dates. **History** retains the transitions.

To find closed work, open **Matters**, use **Filter** and enable **Show closed**, and check any other active filters or saved view. You can also follow an existing Matter link if you still have access.

## Archive or restore

Open **Matter actions** beside the Status strip and select **Archive**. Read the confirmation, and select **Archive** again. Use **Cancel** if you only meant to close normal completed work. Archiving keeps the M- reference and History while removing the Matter from default lists and preventing ordinary changes.

To restore it, find the Matter with **Show archived** in the Matters filters or follow its existing link. If it is also closed, include **Show closed** as well. Open **Matter actions**, select **Restore**, then confirm **Restore** in the dialog.

Restoring makes the Matter editable again; it does not reopen a closed Matter or change the lifecycle of children or linked Contracts. Reopen separately if needed. If a write is refused because another person archived the Matter, read its current state and restore it before trying again. Review [finding work](find-your-work.md) when filters still hide the record.
